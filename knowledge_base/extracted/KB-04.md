Home camera with the ACME Home app. Cloud recording is optional. Sold in Italy, Germany and France.

What the service numbers mean

Snapshot at 22 October: 18,000 active devices and 12,000 accounts with cloud recording enabled. One account can have several devices. These figures are not a count of people affected by any particular event.

Questions we send to Security

For an unfamiliar sign-in, ask for the account reference, device version and the time of the alert. Do not ask customers to send passwords or camera recordings to a public social account. Security checks sessions and logs. Sophie should be involved if personal data may have been accessed.

Data handled by the service

Account email, device identifier, access logs and recordings where cloud recording is enabled. Access to logs and customer recordings is restricted to the people handling the case.

Avoid overpromising

An account recovery is not proof that earlier recordings were never accessed. Security needs to state what was checked, over which period and with what limits.
