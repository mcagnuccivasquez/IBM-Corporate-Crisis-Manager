ACME Company overview

Commercial briefing | updated September 2026

[Extraction note: Photo of an office and an electronics workbench. Illustration only, contains no text.]

We design everyday electronics for homes and mobile work. Our teams in Milan work with manufacturing partners and a European service network.

Where we work

Head office and product validation: Milan. European distribution and service centre: Frankfurt. Assembly partners: Vietnam and Taiwan. Main markets: Italy, Germany and France.

[page 2]

The business in brief

| 2025 revenue | Employees | Sales mix |
|---|---|---|
| EUR 180 million | 620 | 60% power and accessories; 40% connected devices |

These figures describe business divisions, not the revenue or profit of an individual product.

How products reach customers

Customers buy from the ACME online store or authorised retailers. The same products also appear on third-party marketplaces. The commercial team cannot confirm the source of every marketplace listing.

Products currently discussed with retail partners

PowerCharge X: a compact mains charger for laptops and mobile devices. SafeCam Home: a connected camera with optional cloud recording. Product sheets are maintained by the category teams.

Working across the company

Operations coordinates cross-functional problems. Customer Care records customer reports; Quality checks hardware; Security investigates digital issues. Legal and the external DPO advise the relevant teams. Public statements are coordinated by Communications.

This overview was prepared for commercial onboarding. Detailed product, support and stock records are held by the relevant teams.
