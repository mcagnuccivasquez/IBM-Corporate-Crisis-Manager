VN-3 visit note

Maya Chen | Supplier quality | 21 July 2026

Plant: VN-3, Vietnam. Visit on 16-17 July. Product line observed: PowerCharge X, hardware H3.1.

What we saw

Operators demonstrated sensor placement checks and end-of-line load testing. We reviewed the records for 30 sampled units from the July production run. The sampled records were complete and no failed thermal protection test was found in that sample.

Follow-up agreed

The factory will send a clearer link between the serial number, production shift and final test record. Luca needs the same batch identifier in shipping files. This is a records improvement; no product hold was raised during this visit.

Scope

The visit was a supplier process check and a sample review. It was not an independent certification of every charger. September batches were not produced at the time of the visit and were not part of the sample.

Sent to: Elena Rossi, Luca Ferri. Original photos and factory worksheets are held by Quality.
