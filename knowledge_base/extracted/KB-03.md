100 W GaN mains charger. Two USB-C ports and one USB-A port. Available in Italy, Germany and France since January 2026. Hardware H3.1.

For the retail team

The charger can feel warm during sustained laptop charging. Do not use touch alone to diagnose a fault. If a customer reports smoke, sparks, a burning smell or a damaged case, refer the report to Customer Care and Quality rather than sending the standard warmth explanation.

Notes behind the sales copy

Thermal control - engineering summary

Two sensors monitor internal temperatures. Power reduction starts at 65 C internally. Protective shutdown is designed to occur at 75 C, with a design tolerance of +/-2 C. These are internal sensor settings, not surface temperature limits. They describe the design, not a test result for every unit in the field.

Things the charger does not do

PCX-100 has no network connection and no remote firmware update. The H3.1 label is a hardware revision. Do not promise a remote fix for the charger.

Returns and identification

Ask for model, serial, place of purchase and a photo of the label. A matching-looking serial alone is not enough to prove authenticity. Quality may need the physical sample. Customers should not open the unit or try to reproduce a dangerous symptom.

Validation background

Project validation samples passed the December 2025 checks. That does not rule out a later manufacturing issue or a problem in an individual unit.
