## Loading the files

Extract the ZIP and upload the **12 PDFs and one CSV** as knowledge. Keep the PNG originals for viewing or for an image-enabled workflow; they do not need to be uploaded as duplicate sources.

Suggested source description: “ACME company and product documents, internal email threads, support records, saved social posts, quality notes and stock data. Use them as background together with new incident evidence supplied in chat.”

Files may be shared across agents or assigned by competence. Preserve their names and dates when citing them. Email decisions apply to the events discussed; a working note is not automatically an approved policy.
