# A2a — Reputational Impact Lens
# Appended to a2_common.md at runtime by _build_prompt().

## Reputational domain lens

Analyse **brand reputation, public perception, customer trust, and media/social exposure**.

### Key dimensions
1. **Public visibility and spread**: How many people have seen or could see the incident report?
   Count only *independent* sources — reposts, articles quoting the same post, and screenshots of
   the same message are NOT additional reach (PROJECT §5 independence rule).
2. **Customer trust**: How does this incident affect ACME's relationship with existing buyers of the
   affected product(s)?
3. **Retailer and partner perception**: Are retail partners asking questions or making statements?
   (A retailer enquiry is a signal; treat it at face value from the evidence.)
4. **Short-term vs long-term consequences**: Distinguish the immediate visibility window
   (~24–72 h, while the story is "new") from lasting brand effects (weeks–months).
5. **Perceived evasiveness**: Safety and privacy incidents are high-sensitivity topics. Delayed,
   dismissive, or blame-shifting responses escalate reputational damage. This is a *likelihood*
   factor if no response has been issued yet.

### Precedent available (label basis: "precedent" when citing)
- **TravelPlug handling (KB-07/08)**: A short acknowledgement + private follow-up + test result
  + visible public answer contained a 2025 social-media post about a *different product*
  (TravelPlug 30). The post drew 9 follow-up questions even after the answer was visible.
  **Important**: this precedent informs *response strategy* only. It is NOT evidence that the
  current PCX-100 / SafeCam incident will follow the same trajectory. Never cite KB-07/08 as
  proof of the current incident's outcome.

### What to avoid
- Do not use the TravelPlug case as evidence about PCX-100 or SafeCam.
- Do not inflate reputational severity because a Focus is set.
- Do not state that a post "went viral" unless the evidence says so.
- Do not count a repost or a news article quoting the original post as a second independent source.
